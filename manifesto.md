We all feel sometimes like we live in hopeless and despairing times. Climate change, the rise of fascism, the atomization of individuals, exploding inequalities, the repeated failures of left-wing movements…

This harsh assessment is shared by all, felt viscerally by all of those who still believe that another world is possible. The generalized sense of powerlessness that we feel paralyzes us all, and our enemies take advantage of this to grab every day more and more power and wealth.

The question that has always tormented the left is back (if it ever ceased to be relevant): what is to be done?

The answers to this question will always be both multiple and imperfect. We certainly don't affirm to offer a complete answer to this famous "what is to be done," but we believe we might be able to add a small stone to the vast edifice that remains to be built.

We need a concrete way to resist, to organize, and above all make alternatives to capitalism possible.

What if we try to spend our money and time away from capitalism as much as possible? What if we could easily find out about the various resources outside the sacrosanct market in our region with the goal of facilitating their use?

A decentralised, collaborative tool, open to the entire left and with a horizontal organizational structure.

Every dollar and every hour of time that can escape capitalism gives us more resources to create viable alternatives by funding them and sustaining them through our common efforts.

Faced with the ecological crisis and the rise of fascism, and what this means for the rights of women, LGBTQ+ people, immigrants, and all other marginalized groups, it is more than time to stand together. We need to build resilient networks in the real world now.

Do you know of any places that offer self-defense or counter-surveillance classes, that have created a community grocery project in your neighborhood, or that help people install Linux or other free software on their computers? Any emancipatory and anti-capitalist action has its place here.

Welcome to people from all corners of the progressist family. Let’s get free from paralysis now! Let's work to organize our lives outside the dictates of capital, patriarchy, ecocide, and fascists!
